## 摘要
在野生动物种群中，长期污染物暴露可能在明显的人口统计学或健康效应可被检测到之前，就已造成隐性的生理代价。表观遗传时钟可量化生物学衰老，并可能为累积性毒理压力提供敏感的生物标志物。全氟和多氟烷基物质（PFAS）是可在海洋食物网中生物累积的持久性污染物，然而其对野生动物长期生理后果的认识仍然有限。本文检验了 PFAS 暴露是否与普通海豚（Delphinus delphis）生物学衰老加速相关。我们分析了来自新西兰海域 30 头搁浅或兼捕海豚的肝脏 PFAS 浓度和皮肤 DNA 甲基化谱。利用近期建立的物种特异性表观遗传时钟估算表观遗传年龄，并将表观遗传年龄与实际年龄之间残差偏差定义为年龄加速。采用基于信息论的建模框架，我们评估了 PFAS 总负荷、性别及其交互作用对表观遗传年龄加速的影响。PFAS 总浓度与表观遗传年龄加速呈正相关，表明 PFAS 负荷较高的海豚在生物学上比其实际年龄所预期的更衰老。

总 PFAS 每增加 1 ng g⁻¹，生物学年龄平均增加 0.031 年。性别对年龄加速无显著影响，提示 PFAS 相关的衰老效应在两性中均会发生。尽管这一效应幅度不大，但与 PFAS 作为一种慢性生理应激源、影响分子衰老过程的作用一致。我们的研究首次提供了将 PFAS 暴露与野生哺乳动物生物学衰老加速联系起来的证据，凸显了表观遗传衰老可作为评估野生动物长期污染物效应的综合性生物标志物。

## Abstract
Chronic contaminant exposure may impose hidden physiological costs long before obvious demographic or health effects become detectable in wildlife populations. Epigenetic clocks quantify biological ageing and may provide sensitive biomarkers of cumulative toxicological stress. Per-and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) are persistent contaminants that bioaccumulate in marine food webs, yet their long-term physiological consequences for wildlife remain poorly understood. Here, we tested whether PFAS exposure is associated with accelerated biological ageing in common dolphins (Delphinus delphis). We analysed liver PFAS concentrations and skin DNA methylation profiles from 30 stranded or bycaught dolphins from New Zealand waters. Epigenetic age was estimated using a recently developed species-specific epigenetic clock, and age acceleration was calculated as the residual deviation between epigenetic and chronological age. Using an information-theoretic modelling framework, we assessed the effects of total PFAS burden, sex, and their interactions on epigenetic age acceleration. Total PFAS concentrations were positively associated with epigenetic age acceleration, indicating that dolphins with higher PFAS burdens were biologically older than expected for their chronological age.

Each 1 ng g{square}{superscript 1} increase in total PFAS was associated with an average increase of 0.031 years in biological age. Sex did not significantly influence age acceleration, suggesting that PFAS-associated ageing effects occur across both sexes. Although modest, this effect is consistent with PFAS acting as a chronic physiological stressor influencing molecular ageing processes. Our findings provide the first evidence linking PFAS exposure to accelerated biological ageing in a wild mammal, highlighting epigenetic ageing as an integrative biomarker of long-term contaminant effects in wildlife.
