## 摘要
除草剂污染和硝酸盐污染在近岸水体中经常同时出现，但二者对珊瑚的联合作用一直是一个关键的认知空白。尽管珊瑚能够对硝酸盐富集表现出一定的驯化能力，但在与 PSII 类除草剂共同暴露时，这种驯化是否仍能维持尚不清楚。在本研究中，我们结合生理学、超微结构以及宿主—共生体多组学，考察了硝酸盐与 PSII 类除草剂 Prometryn 对造礁珊瑚 Acropora hyacinthus 的联合作用。结果表明，在复合胁迫条件下，Prometryn 是导致共生虫黄藻科（Symbiodiniaceae）发生光损伤和光合作用毒性的主要驱动因素。透射电子显微镜观察显示，Prometryn 诱导了被激活的共生体自噬（symbiophagy），并导致 Symbiodiniaceae 的叶绿体发生降解，类囊体堆叠松散。转录组学和蛋白质组学分析表明，硝酸盐富集刺激了宿主基于谷胱甘肽的抗氧化响应。然而，Prometryn 诱导的光毒性损害了光合产物的供给，并破坏了这种对硝酸盐的驯化机制。此外，光合产物转移的减少进一步损害了宿主的能量平衡，并加剧了氧化胁迫。这些发现表明，PSII 类除草剂能够抵消对硝酸盐富集的短期驯化，凸显了在礁生态系统中对除草剂和营养盐输入进行联合管控的必要性。

## Abstract
Herbicide and nitrate pollution frequently co-occur in coastal waters, while their combined effect on corals has been a critical knowledge gap. Although corals can exhibit acclimation to nitrate enrichment, it is unclear whether this acclimation persists under coexposure to PSII herbicides. Here, we investigated the combined effects of nitrate and the PSII herbicide prometryn on the reef-building coralAcropora hyacinthusby integrating physiology, ultrastructure, and host-symbiont multiomics. The results demonstrate that prometryn acts as the primary driver of photodamage and photosynthetic toxicity in the symbiotic Symbiodiniaceae under costress. Transmission electron microscopy revealed that prometryn induced activated symbiophagy and chloroplast degradation with loosely stacked thylakoids in Symbiodiniaceae. Transcriptomics and proteomics analyses predicted that nitrate enrichment stimulated host glutathione-based antioxidant responses. However, prometryn-induced phototoxicity impaired photosynthate supply and disrupted this nitrate acclimation mechanism. Moreover, reduced photosynthate transfer further impaired the host energy balance and intensified oxidative stress. These findings show that PSII herbicides can override short-term acclimation to nitrate enrichment, underscoring the need for the joint regulation of herbicide and nutrient inputs in reef ecosystems.
